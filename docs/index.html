Mini games

Chat doesn't just watch — it plays

Chat commands trigger events live on your overlay. Winners get crowns, losers get dramatic KOs, and everyone earns points just by hanging out.

Duels

Challenge anyone. Both cats charge, collide mid-air with a screen shake, and chat cheers decide the winner. Loser flops over. Winner wears a golden crown.

!duel @friend

Races

Open a join window, cats line up at the edge, then it's a hopping sprint across your stream — with random trip-flops. Podium for the top 3.

!race  +  !join

Boss battles

Setup

Live in under two minutes

The overlay is hosted right here — no download, no account, no server. It reads public Twitch chat anonymously.

Copy the overlay URL

Swap yourname for your Twitch channel name:

devvgwardo.github.io/nubcat-chat-pets/overlay/index.html?channel=yourname

Add it to OBS

Sources → + → Browser, paste the URL, set width/height to your canvas (e.g. 1920×1080). The background is fully transparent — only cats, labels and bubbles composite onto your stream.

Chat takes over

Viewers appear as they chat, each with their own persistent cat in their Twitch color, and can trigger duels, races and boss battles whenever they want.

Prefer to self-host? Clone the repo and run python3 -m http.server 8741 from the folder — then use http://localhost:8741/index.html?channel=yourname instead.

FAQ

Good questions

Is it really free? What's the catch?
It's free and it runs entirely in your browser. The overlay connects to Twitch's public anonymous read-only chat gateway — no OAuth, no account access, nothing to trust but your own browser.
Does it slow down my stream?
No. It's a lightweight WebGL overlay capped at 40 cats by default (raise with ?max=), particles are hard-capped, and it renders only when OBS shows the source. A 2015 laptop handles it fine.
How do viewers earn points?
Every chat message earns a point. Points tip the odds in duels and show up in the debug HUD. Boss fighters earn bonus points and winners get crowns — all stored locally in the overlay's browser storage.
Can I turn the games off and just have cats?
Yes — add ?games=0 to your OBS URL. You get the pure roaming-cat overlay with hops, hearts and celebration jumps, no game events.
My cats walk backwards. Fix?
Some capture setups mirror the canvas. Add ?flip=1 and they'll face the right way.
The cats aren't showing up in OBS
Check that the URL uses ?channel=yourname exactly and that "Shutdown source when not visible" is off if you want cats to keep roaming while previewing scenes. If you're self-hosting, make sure the local server (python3 -m http.server 8741) is still running.
